# Online sportsbook availability

Verified November 14, 2026. Scope: the ten named operators, across 50 states and DC. This is operator availability, not a general list of where sports betting is legal. A dash means none of these ten offers verified statewide online sports betting there. Territories and Canada are outside this selector.

## Scope and exceptions

Only regulated online/mobile sportsbooks are included. Retail-only, on-property-only mobile wagering, racing, casino, fantasy, and prediction products are excluded. In particular, the BetMGM location page includes racing in Florida and casino-property-only mobile betting in Mississippi; neither makes BetMGM an active statewide online sportsbook there.

Nevada includes BetMGM, Caesars, and Circa mobile wagering; account registration can require an in-person visit. Louisiana has parish restrictions. DC and individual operators can have additional geofenced exclusions. State availability does not override operator location checks or account requirements.

Circa Arizona is not marked active: its [Arizona page](https://www.circasports.com/arizona-app) advertises a January 19 launch without a year and retains a launch-updates form, while its current support list names the other six states. Confirm a live launch before adding AZ.

## App behavior

The active-books row shows availability for all ten operators. Only the four connected feeds (DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, and Caesars) can currently supply comparison results. Other icons say comparisons are coming soon in their tooltip. State selection limits comparison book choices and results; it does not change the collector region or create state-specific odds feeds.

The selector includes only states with active statewide online sports betting, plus DC. Retail-only, on-property-only mobile, and authorized-but-not-launched markets are omitted. Rhode Island remains selectable because its [lottery sportsbook is legal online](https://webserver.rilegislature.gov/statutes/title42/42-61.2/42-61.2-16.HTM), even though it is outside this ten-operator catalog. The full audit matrix above retains all 50 states and DC.

An unset state displays All states, shows all ten catalog operators, and selects all connected comparison books. Clearing a selected state restores those defaults. States with no catalog books show an explicit empty state. States with available operators but no connected feeds explain that their books are not in comparisons yet. Saved individual book preferences remain intact when switching between specific states; the All book choice follows the selected state.
